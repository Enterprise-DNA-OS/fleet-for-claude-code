# Why there is no front end

Fleetio is a database of vehicles, meters, services, fuel and costs, with screens and a phone app on top. The records underneath are ordinary: a few tables and a handful of weekly jobs. What the per-vehicle subscription mostly buys is the layer that lets people who do not write queries get at them.

That layer used to be the whole product. It is not needed for the fleet office any more. Open this folder in Claude Code, ask "what is due for service before the end of the month and which workshop has it", and it runs the query and answers.

## What you gain

- **Questions the dashboard never had.** Cost per km against the vehicle's own fuel history, workshops compared on days to finish, vehicles with a WoF expiring before their next booked service.
- **No per-vehicle fee.** Add the trailer and the forklift without thinking about the plan band.
- **Your records in your database.** Back them up, query them from anything, leave any time.
- **Your rules.** Your service intervals, your RUC habits, your depots, your replacement policy.

## What a screen gives that this does not

Being honest about it:

- **A driver's phone app.** Drivers doing inspections, logging faults with photos, or scanning fuel dockets need a phone screen. The free version records what the office enters.
- **Offline use.** Claude Code needs a connection.
- **Live telematics and fuel card feeds.** The free version takes CSV files. Live connections are a build.
- **A calendar you drag jobs around on.** Work orders are rows you ask about.

Enterprise DNA builds these into your own version when your fleet needs them: a driver app, fuel card and telematics connections, a web front end for the office. That is the customised path.

## Who this fits

Fleet and operations managers of 10 to 200 vehicles who spend their week chasing services, renewals and workshops, and who would rather ask than click. If your drivers live in a phone app today, keep that part and move the office.
